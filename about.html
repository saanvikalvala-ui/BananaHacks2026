About Formivue

Transforming raw imagination into interactive architectural blueprints and 3D models in real time.

Our Mission

Formivue bridges the gap between creative architectural concepts and technical visualization. Whether you're planning a cozy farmhouse, a modern urban office, or a neighborhood cafe, our platform instantly generates structured conceptual floor plans, customizable elevations, and immersive 3D walkthroughs without the steep learning curve of professional CAD software.

The Problem We're Solving

Traditional architecture tools require hours of manual setup, precise scaling configurations, and expensive software licenses just to test a basic layout idea. Formivue lets you describe your vision in plain English and instantly interact with a multi-angle representation of your future space.

Under the Hood

Built for speed and reliability, Formivue combines dynamic SVG geometry generation for detailed floor plans and clean front elevations with a custom Three.js WebGL engine for 3D interior and exterior visualization—all packaged into a lightweight, zero-dependency client application.
